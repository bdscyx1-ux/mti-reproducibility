# Preserved data and reproduction boundaries

Gate13R-AX2 expanded-search block outcomes are missing. The 20 October 2026 cleanup
audit records removal of AX2 block payloads while retaining `_control` and `_commit`.
The archived acquisition receipts do not contain substitute observations. The user
reported on 7 November 2026 that no backup is currently known and asked to complete
the remaining work. No replacement data were collected or fabricated.

The retained `p1-analysis.v1.json` contains campaign-level P1 summaries and inferential
results. These can be inspected as archived aggregates. They do not permit verification
of the original within-campaign records or operation-level telemetry. AX2 P2–P4 and
operation-budget values retained in manuscript tables are expressly labelled as
source-manuscript summaries, not independently reproduced measurements.

Affected displays are Table 3 and Supplementary Tables S1, S2 and S3. Other rows in
those tables are regenerated where the inputs survive. The five manuscript figures
use SMSB/MQ data and are not dependent on AX2 raw blocks.

Five historical offline analyses, supplemental A/B, SMSB scoring, the historical
movement preference score and figure source data can be recomputed. The original
frozen formulas, finite experimental matrix, deterministic zero-variance convention,
confidence intervals and hypothesis families are preserved. A successful numerical
replay does not independently validate the scientific design or generalize beyond
the measured configurations.

The historical standalone movement executable was previously reported unavailable.
The minimal Unity project tests six algorithm cores; it does not restore that
executable or recollect all timed experiments. Current corrected D* Lite source and
historical D* Lite evidence must remain distinguishable.

This is a local preparation, not a public deposition. Do not insert a public URL,
release tag, DOI, or statement that all raw data are available until the actual
release has been made and its scope checked. In particular, a future availability
statement must disclose the AX2 raw-data gap instead of saying the complete raw
dataset is available on request.
