## Pass/fail rules

Every check above is a hard gate. Missing, partial, non-positive, non-finite, hash-mismatched or cardinality-mismatched evidence fails closed. A measured attempt is never resumed or merged. Versioned repair/retry may correct only Gate 12R implementation, Windows PowerShell/Unity compatibility, or orchestration defects; it may not alter the frozen matrix, rows, algorithms, thresholds, reset semantics, precision, no-cap rule, telemetry, exclusion policy, or Playable semantics.

The pre-pilot fixtures and pilot are environment-sensitive for timing, but deterministic structural projections require exact match. Wall-clock values are never compared for reproducibility.
