# Gate 13R-PR2 raw-data / analysis two-phase commit specification v2

## Purpose and amendment status

This v2 specification is an additive, post-failure amendment for formal attempt 3 only. It preserves v1 as historical evidence and changes no scientific estimand, endpoint, contrast, multiplicity rule, schedule, sample target, algorithm, scenario, Williams order, or AB/BA order.

Unlike v1, v2 permits one narrowly bounded administrative continuation after the documented Windows status-persistence failure. This exception was created after the interruption and must never be represented as preregistered or as part of the original non-resumable contract.

During preparation of this amendment, the automated drafting agent opened the ordinal 3,470 block manifest to confirm structural completion; the returned content also exposed algorithm timing fields. No comparison, effect, interval, significance test, ranking, winner, or superiority analysis was performed, and those values are excluded from the amendment. This exposure must be disclosed, and the recovery-readiness verdict must be produced by an independent outcome-blind structural validator.

## Attempt identity across an administrative interruption

For this amendment, one formal attempt is identified by the same formal root, original execution receipt, frozen config and schedule hashes, one global ordinal space, and exactly one measured observation at every scheduled identity. A replacement orchestrator process does not by itself create a new scientific attempt, provided that:

1. no measured block is rerun, duplicated, replaced, or selectively retained;
2. the landed-but-uncommitted ordinal 3,470 is accepted only through a read-only forensic audit;
3. continuation begins at ordinal 3,471 and follows the frozen order through 3,552;
4. the original failure status and evidence remain immutable and disclosed; and
5. the final raw commit proves exact complete coverage and uniqueness.

## Phase 1A — preserved original segment

The original segment contains durably committed ordinals 1–3,469. These files remain byte-preserved. Ordinal 3,469 is one half of matched superblock `g13rpr2-h21-m0007` and cannot enter a cross-cohort matched estimator until ordinal 3,470 is accepted.

The original failure status remains terminal historical evidence. It is not edited to create a fiction of uninterrupted completion.

## Phase 1B — forensic promotion of ordinal 3,470

Ordinal 3,470 already produced a complete on-disk block, but the failed orchestrator did not create its process-identity commit. A separately authorized read-only forensic audit may create an external commit-sidecar only after verifying the complete evidence chain from frozen schedule and block plan through manifest, checkpoint, sample hashes, memory gates, wrapper summary/log, PID/start-time, and Unity exit.

The audit may not modify the formal payload, manufacture missing identity fields, infer a value from scientific outcomes, or rerun Unity. If the identity chain cannot be closed exactly, recovery fails and attempt 3 remains incomplete.

## Phase 1C — one administrative continuation

After a passing recovery-readiness checkpoint and a separate launch authorization, one and only one continuation segment may execute ordinals 3,471–3,552. It must use strict serial execution, one fresh Unity process per block, the original frozen schedule order, and a versioned status-persistence implementation validated against concurrent monitor reads.

No ordinal at or below 3,470 may be run again. No ordinal above 3,552 may be added. A measured hard failure in the continuation stops the attempt; this specification does not authorize another continuation.

## Phase 1 final raw commit

`RAW-DATA-COMPLETE / ANALYSIS-PENDING / IMMUTABLE` may be declared only after a full read-only audit proves:

- exactly 3,552 unique scheduled processes;
- exactly 10,176 unique scheduled samples;
- exactly 1,008 complete heterogeneous matched superblocks;
- all shared Williams blocks and all heterogeneous AB/BA and row quotas;
- no duplicate global ordinal, run ID, sample identity, or measured block;
- every required correctness, structural, memory, telemetry, hash, persistence, license, environment, and process-identity gate;
- a stable canonical raw-payload tree across two reads; and
- no measured-payload mutation caused by recovery.

The final qualified label is:

`ONE-COMPLETE-FORMAL-ATTEMPT-SAME-ROOT-ADMINISTRATIVELY-INTERRUPTED-POSTFAILURE-AMENDED`

Every report must also state that the original acquisition was interrupted and that this exception was adopted after the failure. The unqualified claims “uninterrupted,” “completed under the original no-resume contract,” and “preregistered recovery” are prohibited.

## Phase 2 — analysis commit

Formal statistical analysis remains a separate authorization. A successful Phase 1 final commit makes ordinals 1–3,552 eligible for the original estimands, subject to the analysis implementation's ordinary block/sample eligibility checks.

Ordinal 3,469 enters cross-cohort heterogeneous inference only together with the forensically accepted ordinal 3,470. Before final Phase 1 completion, this specification does not authorize partial formal inference. Any proposed analysis of the already complete shared cohort or truncated heterogeneous cohort requires a separate, explicitly post-failure statistical amendment and cannot claim that the full Attempt-3 acquisition was complete.

Analysis must remain read-only against the frozen raw root, use isolated versioned analysis attempts, and verify raw-tree hash equality before and after execution. Gate 14R remains unauthorized.

## Current stop condition

This specification authorizes no Unity execution and no statistical analysis. Work stops after the versioned contract artifacts are written. Forensic readiness work and administrative continuation each require their own explicit authorization and gates.
