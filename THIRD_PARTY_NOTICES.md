# Third-party materials and publication boundary

The project author previously confirmed authorship of the original project scripts; research extensions, analysis adapters and figure generators are distributed with the author-owned code under the license described in LICENSING.md. No third-party source copyright/license header was identified in the selected C#, Python, C++ or header-file snapshot. This is a source-provenance review, not a grant of rights to referenced dependencies.

## Dependencies installed separately

Unity Editor and Unity package dependencies are not redistributed. Package manifests record dependencies; users obtain them separately under their respective terms. Python, NumPy, PyArrow and Matplotlib are installed separately; their source and binaries are not bundled here. The minimal Unity project tests algorithm functionality and does not reconstruct the complete game or every timed acquisition.

## Materials not bundled

Commercial/origin-uncertain visual assets, scene/prefab payloads, third-party font/sprite assets, Unity binaries, the referenced dissertation PDF and manuscript DOCX files are excluded. Archived records that mention a visual prefab are provenance, not a distribution of that prefab or a claim that it can be recreated from this package.

Three helper scripts copied from a local figure-review toolkit had no established redistribution license. They are excluded from this public copy. Figure generation now uses a newly written, study-specific layout check in tools/check_figure_layout.py. Earlier QA reports remain historical outputs; they do not imply that the external toolkit is distributed or licensed here.

Machine-bound encrypted label-custody files and their entropy payload are also excluded. The public historical movement route uses the already released label map and does not decrypt those files. Hash commitments are retained as provenance. See docs/PUBLICATION_EXCLUSIONS.json for exact exclusions and hashes; original local evidence is preserved.

References and attributions in scientific content remain with their respective authors. The MIT and CC BY grants apply only as described in LICENSING.md.
